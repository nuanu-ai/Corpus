# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus discussion on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through a channel listed on the repository profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
